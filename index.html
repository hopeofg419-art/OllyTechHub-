The problem

Most businesses are not losing sales because the work is bad. They lose sales in forty DMs, a screenshot of an account number, and a customer who quietly decides not to risk it.

No way to take payment. No record of who asked what. And often a pretty site that does nothing — no checkout, no lead capture, no dashboard. That is the gap between how good a business is and how legit it looks.
